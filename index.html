Automated Support Ticket Routing Across 6 Connected Tools Using Zapier and AI

The problem

Support requests had to be triaged by hand.

What I did

I built a Zapier workflow that captures support requests through Google Forms and records each ticket in Google Sheets. It categorizes tickets, assigns a priority and uses ChatGPT to draft a first response. Based on priority, each ticket goes down one of three Slack notification paths, so the right team can act faster.

The result

One routing system connects 6 business tools and runs 3 priority paths: High, Medium and Low. Requests are categorized, prioritized, logged and routed with no manual triage.

AI-Powered Lead Qualification Workflow for Agency Teams

The problem

Leads had to be qualified and sorted by hand.

What I did

I built an n8n workflow that reads incoming lead data with ChatGPT, assigns a qualification score and updates the tracking records. It routes each lead into Hot, Warm or Cold. Qualified prospects trigger team notifications and personalized follow-up actions with no manual steps.

The result

One process connects 5 business systems and 3 lead tiers. Lead qualification is standardized, visibility improves and there is less manual lead sorting.
